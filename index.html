Important things to know.

Read these before sharing or relying on your results.

Does this calculate net pay?

Yes, optionally. It uses a clearly labeled scenario based on published Kenyan tax and statutory deduction rules around November 2026. It is illustrative, particularly for later CBA years. Your payslip, KRA and pension scheme arrangements control.

Is KSh 18,000 commuter allowance guaranteed immediately?

No. The agreement lists a commuter target of KSh 18,000 per month for Grades 5–15, but permits progressive implementation over up to three years for universities currently paying below that rate. Enter the amount you expect your institution to implement.

Are commuter and annual leave arrears included?

No. Allowance arrears are excluded because retrospective effective rates and phased implementation need confirmation. Annual leave allowance is shown separately as a once-yearly amount, not added to monthly gross pay.

Why could my arrears be different?

Actual entitlement depends on starting notch, increment date, promotions, changes in grade, actual payments, salary already implemented and payroll reconciliation. The reference assumption is not proof of what an employee was paid.

When does the return-to-work agreement say arrears should be paid?

It provides for arrears from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026 to be paid in three equal instalments without specifying their individual dates, and the arrears from 1 July to 31 December 2026 together with January 2027 salary. Funding and employer implementation must still be confirmed.

Where do the salary figures come from?

The 30 October 2026 Return-to-Work Formula between IPUCCF/FKE and KUSU, specifically the salary schedule on pages 9–14 and the allowance schedule on page 8. This independent tool is not an official interpretation of the agreement.

How are promotions and departures treated?

A promotion resets the grade and notch from the selected effective month. The chosen increment month continues afterwards, but no separate increment is applied in the same month as the promotion. If you leave, arrears accumulate only through your final month of employment. Promotions may make the previous-year reference comparator unsuitable; enter actual basic pay for accurate comparisons.

Does "first month new salary is paid" change the CBA effective date?

No. The CBA schedule is applied from July 2025. The implementation field only estimates when your payslip began reflecting the revised salary and therefore when unpaid basic salary differences stop accumulating. Any actual-pay entries override the assumption.
